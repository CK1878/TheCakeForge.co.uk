Operating Procedures & Safety Protocol

Location: Carlisle, Cumbria | Registered Business: The Cake Forge

1. Kitchen Environment & Child/Domestic Isolation

The Cake Forge operates from a dedicated domestic kitchen in rented accommodation within Carlisle. To maintain absolute food safety separation:

  • Child Safety & Access: Commercial production is strictly restricted to times when children are at school or in bed, ensuring zero domestic interference during active baking, decorating, or packaging sessions.
  • Countertop & Storage Clearance: All non-baking domestic items are cleared from countertops and stored in designated storage containers outside the kitchen prior to production.
  • Dedicated Storage Zones: All business food storage, dedicated business fridge, and freezer units are housed within the kitchen. Business cupboards are sealed and clean. Perishable or food-contact items are stored in sealed containers or labelled, ziplocked bags.

2. Facility Management: Single Sink, Dishwasher & Waste

Operating with a single kitchen sink requires rigorous time-sequencing and mechanical sanitisation:

  • Time-Sequenced Sink Usage: Handwashing is strictly separated from equipment washing. Handwashing uses the dedicated sink equipped with compliant antibacterial soap and a blue roll dispenser.
  • Mechanical Dishwashing: Heavy utensils, mixing bowls, and equipment are processed via the domestic dishwasher operating on high-temperature sanitising cycles, separating food prep wash cycles from household items.
  • Waste Management: A touch-free motion bin is positioned in a corner against non-used cupboards during baking operations (with household bins stored inside cupboards). The commercial waste bin is emptied and sanitized at the end of every operational shift.

3. Staff Roles, Personal Hygiene & PPE

The business is operated by the Primary Baker (holding an active Level 2 Food Hygiene Certificate) and the Secondary Baker (husband assisting intermittently), both adhering to identical EHO-compliant digital workflows:

  • Operator Identification: All digital logs, cold holding checks, and batch entries are tagged on the Kitchen Console using designated operator profiles ("Primary Baker" / "Secondary Baker").
  • PPE & Workwear: Blue detectable plasters, hair nets, and dedicated baking aprons are worn during all production shifts. Laundry of work aprons and headwear is washed separately from family items.

4. Digital Traceability & Natasha’s Law Compliance

The Cake Forge utilizes a custom web-based software ecosystem hosted on GitHub and synced via Firebase Firestore for end-to-end traceability:

  • Inventory Scanning & Allergens: Incoming raw ingredients are scanned via barcode or added via online cross-reference into the Inventory Hub. The system automatically tracks the 14 mandatory EU/UK allergens.
  • Batch Traceability: Cooked components (sponges, ganaches, buttercreams) are logged in the Kitchen Console, linking opened raw ingredient lots directly to the final product.
  • Natasha’s Law & Thermal Labelling: Finished cakes and boxed portions generate direct thermal safety labels via Bluetooth or paper fallback, detailing exact item descriptions, lot numbers, opening dates, Period-After-Opening (PAO) expiry dates, storage instructions, and full allergen declarations.
  • Safety Lockouts: Software guardrails enforce mandatory action logs if fridge temperatures deviate outside 0°C–5.0°C parameters or if out-of-date (OOD) / recalled products are detected.
